Hi, my name's Santiago and I'm a Data Scientist / ML Engineer living in Paris . I did a PhD in Deep Learning where I worked with Graph Neural Networks (GNN) to reconstruct events on a particle physics experiment (KM3NeT). My work has been mainly oriented in high performant MLOps for HPC as well as some DevOps for optimization of the pipelines. I also served as the young researchers representative of the experiment as well as the co-convener of the neutrino interactions joint working group with the IceCube neutrino experiment (you may recognize it as the experiment that gave the Physics Nobel prize in 2026). I have also taught python courses at Université Paris Cité.

Feel free to reach out so we can discuss further possibilites of working together.

I am most skilled in: <mark>Python</mark>, <mark>C++</mark> and the more niche <mark>Julia</mark>.
