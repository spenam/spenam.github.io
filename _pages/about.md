My core stack is <mark>Python</mark>, <mark>PyTorch</mark>, <mark>SQL</mark>, <mark>Docker</mark>, <mark>AWS</mark>, <mark>MLflow</mark> and <mark>CI/CD</mark>, with hands-on work in <mark>LLM/NLP/RAG</mark> workflows, I also use <mark>C++</mark> and <mark>Julia</mark>. I’ve co-led an international team of a dozen of researchers and taught Python and algorithms at Université Paris Cité. I’m looking for Data Scientist / AI Engineer roles where I can turn large, messy datasets into reliable models and production systems. Feel free to reach out, I’d be glad to discuss how we could work together and how I can contribute to your projects.
