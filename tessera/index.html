Tessera

An interactive exploration of mathematical tilings.

I really like mathematical art, so I built a package to play around with tessellations. On this site you can explore periodic, aperiodic and uniform tilings generated by Tessera. Change the parameters to see how the geometry and appearance of each tiling respond.
